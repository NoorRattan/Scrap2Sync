# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Scrap2Sync respectful, safe, and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or level
of technical expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and do not share another person's private information.
- Accept correction gracefully and focus on repairing harm.
- Keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and publication of private
information are not acceptable. Spam, malicious code, secret disclosure, and
using project spaces to target others are also prohibited.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public. Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, lock, or ban contributions and
participants when necessary to protect the community. Enforcement decisions
should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid exposing reporters.

Report conduct concerns to the repository owner through an available private
GitHub contact channel. If none is available, open a minimal issue asking for a
private channel without including names, evidence, or incident details. Security
vulnerabilities should instead follow `SECURITY.md`.
